WHAT THIS TRACE DOES NOT ESTABLISH — read before using any frame

  1. No frame shows an act of lifting a price. #23 is a fact without an author.
  2. #24 shows a transfer, not a motive. Fund managers chose to buy and may be right.
  3. McGoldrick's cost basis is unknown. #24 cannot be turned into a gain figure.
  4. #02 is excluded. It is an unconcluded tribunal and no frame below depends on it.
  5. The NHTSA review concerns Lane Keep Assist, a DIFFERENT system from the chassis and
     suspension components described in that report. They are not the same thing.
  6. #20 carries a counter-fact: cash generation is strong. A trace that reported only what
     suited its author would be worth nothing.
  7. Figures come from a data service compiling exchange filings and from press reports. The
     FY26 annual report sits behind a server refusing requests. Nothing here was read from a
     filing first-hand.

Research, not advice on where to put money. No target prices, no recommendations, no holding
in anything named. Nothing alleges wrongdoing by any person or company, and no claim is made
about what anybody intended.
