ASK Automotive Limited — Kuldip Singh Rathee (Chairman & Managing Director)

₹1,996 crore taken out by the founding family since 2021 — two buybacks that went entirely to the Chairman and consumed 89% and 90% of those years' profits, an IPO that was 100% offer-for-sale and sent the company nothing, two secondary sales, and a decade of rent, remuneration and related-party trade. Company borrowings rose 8.5x over the same period. Before listing the family took about ₹71 crore a year out of company cash; after listing, about ₹838 crore a year out of the market. The largest shareholder is the Chairman's wife, who has never sold a share and has never appeared on an earnings call.
