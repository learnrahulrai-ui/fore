A running, primary-source-only record of what listed-company promoters actually did, cross-referenced against what the public actually earned. No entry predicts what a stock does next; each one reports what already happened and what it cost.

ASK Automotive Limited — Kuldip Singh Rathee (Chairman & Managing Director)

Two confirmed sales since IPO (Sep 2024, Nov 2025), both foretold to the market a year in advance and never once discussed again afterward across eight consecutive earnings calls. The one window the public won outright is the one before either sale happened; every window starting on a sale date has lost to gold and, on all but one, to inflation.
