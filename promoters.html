ASK Automotive Limited — Kuldip Singh Rathee (Chairman & Managing Director)

₹1,933 crore taken out by the founding family since 2021 — two pre-IPO buybacks that consumed 89% and 90% of those years' profits, an IPO that was 100% offer-for-sale and sent the company nothing, and two secondary sales. Company borrowings rose 8.5x over the same period. Includes a 9.4% stake that moved into the promoter's own name after listing and was then sold for ₹911.7 crore, leaving his holding unchanged. Earnings per share have grown 142% since listing; the price 83%.
